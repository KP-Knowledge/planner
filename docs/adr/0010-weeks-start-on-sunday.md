# Weeks start on Sunday, while the work week is still Monday–Friday

In the Gantt week zoom, each week segment starts on Sunday and is labelled with that Sunday's date in the active locale (for example `Nov 8` / `8 พ.ย.`). The Holidays calendar also starts its weeks on Sunday. This replaced the original ISO weeks (Monday start, `W45` labels) at the user's request, to match Instagantt and the wall calendars the team uses. Saturday and Sunday are still non-working days.

## Consequences

- The app shows no week numbers. ISO week numbers don't fit Sunday-start weeks, and US week numbering would confuse people.
- A Sunday-start week has non-working days at both ends, one on each side of the five workdays.
